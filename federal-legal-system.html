How the U.S. Federal Legal System Works—and Why It Matters

A practical introduction to the federal legal system: how the courts fit together, who has power, and why the structure matters for democracy and everyday life.

About this session

Learn how the federal judicial system works, Diveinto two high-profile cases as real-world examples on how political viewpoints are pushed through the legal system and how to defend against this. You will learn about the structure of the courts, how cases move through the system, and what's at stake for our rights and our democracy.
