Action Labs!

A political salon—and a hands-on antidote to doom-scrolling.

Come together in person. Meet your neighbors. Learn how the U.S. government actually works, unpack key points of law, and explore the forces shaping our democracy.

Part political salon, part democracy workshop, Action Labs takes activism offline—with real conversation, real community, and fresh cookies.

You'll walk away with a deeper understanding of the issues, how our institutions really work, and concrete ways you can take action to defend democracy.
