About Action Labs

A political salon and a hands-on antidote to doom-scrolling.

Action Labs brings people together in person to understand how government, law, institutions, technology, and political power actually work — and then figure out what ordinary people can do about it.

Learn something.
Talk about it.
Figure out what to do.
Then do it together.

Why we exist

Understanding is the beginning. Action is the point.

We live in a world of nonstop political news, outrage, commentary, and algorithms competing for our attention. But knowing that something happened is not the same as understanding why it happened, who has power over it, or what can be done next.

Action Labs creates a place to slow down, learn together, ask questions, challenge assumptions, and turn complicated civic and political issues into something people can actually understand.

Then we move beyond understanding. Every Action Lab is designed to help people identify meaningful ways to participate, organize, and take action together.

How Action Labs works

Learn. Discuss. Act.

Each Action Lab combines learning, conversation, and practical action in an informal setting designed for real people rather than experts.

1

Learn

Get past the headlines. Understand how government, law, institutions, technology, and political power actually work.

2

Discuss

Talk it through with real people, in person. Ask questions, challenge assumptions, and hear different perspectives.

3

Act

Leave with something concrete you can do — and the knowledge and materials to help bring other people along.
